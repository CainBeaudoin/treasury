Rules

Human-readable treasury rules. Cards are intentionally compact so the page scans quickly.

USDC Cashback80%

Returned in USDC only when a USDC-funded crate is cashed out.

Credit Back80%

Returned in Credits only when a Credits-funded crate is cashed out.

Crate Bonus5%

New Credits emitted on every crate, whether paid with USDC or Credits.

Marketplace Fee1%

Chosen records only its fee as revenue, not the full marketplace sale.

Lulu Single100 Credits

Per single Lulu burned.

Lulu Triple333 Credits

Every group of three includes a 33 Credit bonus.

Important: Cashback, Credit Back, and Crate Bonus are three different metrics. Cashback is USDC outflow. Credit Back is Credits returned after a Credits-funded cashout. Crate Bonus is a 5% new-credit emission.
