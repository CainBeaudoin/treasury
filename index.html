Rules

Compact operational rules for how theoretical Credits and prizes become real treasury movement.

Credit UnitNon-cash

Credits carry no guaranteed USD value.

Credit Spend− Entry

A Credits-funded crate removes the entry Credits.

Crate Bonus5%

New Credits on every crate, USDC or Credits.

Credit Back80% of prize FMV

Credits-funded settlement based on outcome value.

USDC Cashback80% of prize FMV

USDC-funded settlement; never paid in Credits.

Vaulted ItemPending

No fallback or cash outflow while vaulted.

Physical RedemptionActual cost basis

Real acquisition cost becomes USDC outflow.

365-Day ExpiryAuto fallback

80% of live FMV in original funding asset.

ShippingSeparate

User payment in; carrier/handling cost out.

Marketplace Fee1%

Only Chosen's fee is company revenue.

Lulu Single100 Credits

Per Lulu burned.

Lulu Triple333 Credits

Every three includes a 33 Credit bonus.

Accounting boundary: a vaulted prize can have FMV, fallback exposure and expected cost basis without any USDC moving. USDC outflow is realized only when the item is physically redeemed; expiry instead settles in the original funding asset.
